# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

This repository is a learning environment. People post questions they are worried are stupid,
designs they are not sure about, and results that did not work. That takes courage, and
protecting it is the whole job of this document.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

**Behaviour that builds this place:**

- Answering the question that was asked, then saying what you would have asked instead
- Saying "I don't know" and "I was wrong about that"
- Critiquing the work, with a reason and preferably a measurement
- Assuming a confusing question comes from unclear docs rather than a careless reader
- Welcoming negative results — "I tried it and it did not clear the noise band" is a
  contribution

**Behaviour that does not:**

- Making someone feel stupid for a beginner question. Everyone here was new to retrieval once,
  including the people who wrote it.
- "Just read the docs." If the docs answered it, they were not findable — that is a docs issue,
  not a user error.
- Sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting comments, personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Confidently asserting things you have not measured, in a repository whose entire subject is
  measurement

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any cohort session
where this material is used — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **the address in CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md**. All complaints
will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the
reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or
permanent repercussions determined by other members of the project's leadership.

### Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning with clarity about the violation.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour; no interaction with those
   involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban** — from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — from any public interaction within the community.
